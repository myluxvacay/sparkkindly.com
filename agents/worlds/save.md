## Who it is for
Everyone 13 and over who wants to feel calmer about money: teenagers with their first pocket money or part-time job, students, and adults setting up a budget. "Spend smart. Keep more." is the brand idea: small, practical money habits, explained plainly. This is general education, never personal financial advice; the site shows "General information, not financial advice. Check the details with the provider before you decide." on every Save page.

## Voice
Plain, calm and practical. Explain how something works and what to check, never what the reader should buy. No shame about spending and no pressure to save; no comparing the reader with others. Use worked examples with round, made-up numbers that are clearly illustrative.

## What to produce
- Guides (audience `all`): how a budget works, how saving goals work, how interest is calculated in general, how to read a price, a contract or a subscription before signing up, how to spot a scam or a pressure sale. Sourced facts need sources the reviewer can open (official guidance, regulators, consumer bodies).
- Quizzes (audience `all`): one clear question with a computable or sourced answer, for example working out a unit price or the total of a monthly subscription over a year.
- "How this offer works" explainers (audience `adults`, only when the brief names an offer the owner has listed): what kind of product it is, what to check in its terms, who it may not suit, and that the reader should check the details with the provider. Cite the provider's own terms page as a source. Write no link to the offer and no call to buy: the only link is the one the owner attaches through a sponsorship, with its disclosure added by the site.
- No time-dependent facts in the text: no prices, rates, fees, end dates, "this week" or "limited time". Describe what to look for instead ("check the APR on the provider's page").
- Check the brief's recent titles; do not repeat a premise.

## Never
- personal financial advice, or telling the reader what to do with their own money, debts or tax
- recommending or naming a specific investment, loan, credit card or other credit product, broker, bank account or app as the one to choose
- cryptocurrency, trading tips, "guaranteed" or risk-free returns, get-rich or passive-income claims
- an offer, product, provider or link the brief did not name, or any affiliate or referral link written into the text
- gambling, betting, alcohol, tobacco and vaping, weapons, adult content, payday lending, supplements and weight-loss products, political or religious bodies, or anything marketed to children
- prices, interest rates, fees, deadlines, end dates or any other time-dependent figure in the item text
- an `adults` offer explainer aimed at teenagers, or any Save item rated `kids`
- shame, guilt or fear about money, or comparing the reader with others
- legal, tax or medical advice, or anything about a real private individual
