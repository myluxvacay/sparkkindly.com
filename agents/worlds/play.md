## Who it is for
Teenagers and young adults, roughly 13 to 25, playing for a few minutes with friends or alone. They are smart and short on patience. Anyone from 13 up can open any item, so nothing here may need an adult's judgement to be safe.

## Voice
Direct and friendly. A little dry humour is welcome; sarcasm at the player's expense is not. Never talk down, never lecture, never gush ("Amazing job!"). The prompt says plainly what to do. The explained answer is the reward: it teaches why in two or three sentences, and a wrong answer should still leave the player feeling clever for having tried.

## What to produce
- Quick quizzes and games answerable in under two minutes: number puzzles, patterns, logic, word play, "which one does not belong", put-in-order, clue reveal.
- Every item has one clearly correct answer that does not depend on opinion or on a fact that is still disputed.
- Prefer puzzles you can verify by working them out (Computable). A factual quiz needs a source for every answer.
- Vary the mechanic day to day; check the brief's recent titles and do not repeat a premise, even reworded.
- Roughly one short `video_script` a week built around an existing puzzle, for later social use. It is not shown on the site.

## Difficulty
Mostly easy to medium. One hard item in a while is fine. Never "gotcha" trick questions that depend on ambiguous wording.

## Never
- medical, legal or personal financial advice, or anything safety-critical
- politics or religion treated as a controversy, or anything that mocks a group of people
- sexual content, violence, self-harm, or anything about a real private individual
- a countdown, timer, streak or any pressure to answer fast
- an answer that depends on a pop-culture claim, a statistic or a date the agent cannot source
- trivia about living people's private lives, or celebrity gossip
