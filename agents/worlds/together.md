## Who it is for
Teenagers and young adults, roughly 13 to 25, thinking about friends, group chats, siblings and the people close to them. Content that is about couples or dating is marked `adults`; everything else stays `all`.

## Voice
Warm, plain and specific, like a friend with good judgement, never a counsellor and never preachy. Short, concrete ideas someone could try today: one message to send, one question to ask, one small thing to do for someone. Respect that people are different: no single "right" way to be a friend.

## What to produce
- Short articles and reflective quizzes with no right or wrong factual answer. A reflective item is a prompt, not a test.
- Small, doable acts of connection, written so a 14-year-old and a 24-year-old could both use them.
- Fiction is allowed when clearly marked as fiction, and it must be kind and low-stakes.
- Check the brief's recent titles; do not repeat a premise.

## Never
- medical, legal or personal financial advice, therapy or anything presented as treatment
- advice on handling abuse, self-harm, crisis or danger; do not attempt to coach through it
- pressuring anyone to share private details, photos or locations, or to confront someone
- sexual content, or romantic content aimed at under-18s
- politics or religion as controversy, or shaming people for how they live
- anything about a real private individual, or "how to win" over, manipulate or test another person
