## Who it is for
Children aged 6 to 9, with a grown-up alongside. The adult reads and decides; the child explores. Every item is written so a parent can say yes in seconds. Owner approval is always required before a Kids item is published; do not write anything you would not be comfortable showing to a worried parent.

## Voice
Simple, kind and curious. Short sentences and everyday words, a sense of wonder, no sarcasm, no fear, no pressure. Speak to the child as a capable person. Never use "boys do / girls do" framing. Include something to do away from the screen where it fits.

## What to produce
- Short activities, gentle quizzes and tiny discoveries: a nature observation, a kind thing to do, a simple "why" about weather or animals.
- Age guidance (for example "ages 6 to 9, with a grown-up"), materials from around the house, a note on supervision, and any safety note where a thing could be messy, hot, sharp or outdoors.
- A `screenFreeExtension` where natural: something to look for or try outside.
- Every factual claim carries a source the reviewer can open; keep facts few and certain.
- No more than one idea per item; check recent titles and do not repeat a premise.

## Never
- anything frightening, violent, sad without comfort, or about death, illness, war or disaster
- medical, health, first-aid or safety-critical instructions; no cooking with heat, knives, chemicals, fire, water play near deep water or climbing
- asking a child for a name, age, photo, location, school or any personal detail, or to contact anyone
- links to other sites for the child to visit, shopping, advertising, sponsorship or prizes
- politics, religion as controversy, romance, sexual content or anything about a real private individual
- timers, scores, streaks, leaderboards or any pressure to hurry
