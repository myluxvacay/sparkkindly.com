# Owner-supplied SparkKindly identity

Original `SparkKindly Heart Badge.png` and
`SparkKindly_ Brighter Minds, Kinder Tomorrows.png` were supplied by the owner on
17 October 2026 with a request to use them in the site. Source files are retained
unchanged in source/. See manifest.json for SHA-256 hashes, sizes and provenance.

- sparkkindly-logo.png / badge-128.webp: complete proportionally resized badge.
- icon-32/64/180/192/512.png: original heart and spark only, centered on white;
  tiny outer-ring remnants excluded at the two top corners. No generated redraw.
- banner-640/1280/1983.webp: entire uncropped banner for responsive display.
- social-banner.jpg: entire banner contained within a 1200×630 cream canvas.

Reproduce derivatives with `node --import tsx scripts/prepare-brand.ts`.

The banner is a brand illustration, not documentary evidence. Its lettering is
not used as the website’s only heading/navigation. Kids guidance remains ages
6–9 with an adult, despite the broad general-audience phrase in the artwork.
Owner provision and requested use are recorded; independent copyright or subject
release verification is not claimed. Final rights approval remains a launch step.
