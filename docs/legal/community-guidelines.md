# SparkKindly community guidelines (draft)

> **Status: Draft for professional review. Not legal advice. Not published.**
> Prepared 4 November 2026 by the builder. **Applies when these features launch.** Comments, friend circles,
> communities and contributions are planned, not yet available (spec Projects 4 to 8). Nothing in this draft describes
> a feature that exists today. Sources are in HTML comments.
> See also the [terms draft](terms.md) and the [privacy policy draft](privacy-policy.md).

## Why these guidelines

SparkKindly is for curiosity and kindness, for people of all ages. These guidelines will apply to everything people
share here once comments, circles, communities and contributions are available.
<!-- Sources: spec section 1; Project 4 (circles), comments and moderation, Project 8 (contributions). -->

## Be kind

- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No bullying, harassment, threats, hate or put-downs, including about someone's age, background, body or beliefs.
- No pressure on anyone to share personal details, join a circle or keep playing.

## Keep it safe

- Never post anyone's personal details: full name, address, school, phone number, email, photos or location. This
  matters most for children.
- No sexual content, violence, self-harm content, or anything that could put someone in danger.
- If something worries you, use the report link. Urgent reports are hidden right away until the owner has looked at
  them.
<!-- Sources: spec 4a.4 excluded topics; 4a.6 (urgent safety report hidden on receipt, owner within 24 hours). -->

## Keep it honest

- Do not pretend to be someone else.
- Do not post spam, advertising or links to make money.
- Only contribute work that is yours, or that you have permission to share, and say where facts come from.
<!-- Sources: spec 4a.3 (sponsorship only through the owner); Project 8 contributions; 4a.4 sourcing. -->

## Keep it on topic

Comments are for the item they sit under. No politics or religion as controversy, and no medical, legal or financial
advice.
<!-- Source: spec 4a.4 risk tiers ("Excluded"). -->

## Who can do what (planned)

| | Friend circles | Public comments | Public communities | Contribute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Child profile (under 13) | planned: through the adult's account | no | no | no |
| 13 to 15, consent confirmed | yes | no | no | no |
| 16 and 17 | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| 18 and over | yes | yes | yes | yes |

Kids items never have comments, for anyone.

## What happens if someone breaks these guidelines

Comments are checked before or after they appear. We may hide or remove anything that breaks these guidelines, and may
suspend or close the account that posted it. Repeated or serious problems mean the account is closed.

## For parents and guardians

Children cannot comment or contribute, and nobody can contact a child through SparkKindly. When circles launch, a child
profile's display name and scores will be visible only to members of circles the adult joins them to.

## Open questions for the professional

1. **Moderation duties for a service with teen members**, including what a children's online safety law in
   [jurisdiction] requires of comment moderation and reporting.
2. **Child profiles in circles.** Spec 4a.5 lets circle members see a child's display name and scores; confirm this is
   acceptable before Project 4.
3. **Appeals.** The draft gives no appeal route for removed content or closed accounts; decide whether one is needed.
